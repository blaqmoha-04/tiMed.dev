About Me

Hi, I'm Sadik Tijani Mohammed, a web developer interested in how technology works and what people can build with it.

My journey into web development started when I had the opportunity to join NiV, a social enterprise organisation in Northern Ghana that works to improve the economic opportunities available to young people.

NiV creates opportunities through practical skills training, business incubation, mentorship and business support. One of its initiatives is the Skills Training and Economic Prosperity (STEP) Project, which helps young people gain practical skills they can use in employment and entrepreneurship.

I became interested in web development through the Step to Code programme. That was where I got my first proper introduction to building websites. I learned the basics of HTML, CSS and JavaScript under the guidance of Mr. Nurudeen. Before then, I had an interest in technology, but the programme gave me a clearer idea of what I could actually do with it.

After the programme, I continued learning on my own. The Odin Project has been one of the resources I've found particularly useful. I also follow YouTube tutorials, read documentation and spend time experimenting with code. I don't just want to follow instructions and get something working; I want to understand why it works.

Most of my attention is currently on front-end development. I've been working with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, TypeScript, React, Next.js and Tailwind CSS. I'm also exploring the back end, including Node.js, Express.js, PostgreSQL and REST APIs.

I enjoy taking an idea, breaking it down and gradually turning it into something that works. Some things take longer than expected, and sometimes I have to go back and figure out what went wrong. That's part of the process I'm learning to appreciate.

I know I still have a lot to learn, but that's what keeps me interested in software development. I enjoy building things, figuring out how they work and finding ways to improve them. I want to keep challenging myself, work on more meaningful projects and grow into a developer who can turn ideas into useful applications.
